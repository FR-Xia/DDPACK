Current measured evidence
=========================

The raw records, tables, figures and source manifest describe the release
sources. Measurement date, machine and runtime settings are in the environment
records and measurement_context.txt. Runs use three warmups and five measured
solves per case/language, MATLAB then C++ without concurrent solver or build
jobs. Full local measurement logs are not needed by users to reanalyze the
distributed raw trajectories and timings.
